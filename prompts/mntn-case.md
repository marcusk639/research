## REQUIRED DELIVERABLE FORMAT

1. **Integrated read** (3–6 sentences): what the attorney/employer profiles + base rates + case structure jointly imply, and how they constrain the extremes.
2. **Outcome scenarios**, banded by probability and summing to ~100%. For each: the cash range (weeks/months of pay), the implied full package (COBRA + neutral reference + non-disparagement + release + characterization/UI treatment), the conditions that produce it, and a probability band. Cover at minimum: most-likely middle; a lower-but-real outcome; an upper-edge outcome; and a no-deal/impasse tail (with where the tail leads — demand letter, EEOC/TWC charge — not a cliff).
3. **Timeline scenarios**, banded by probability: time to agreement-in-principle and to signed release, with conditions for each.
4. **Key variables / sensitivities**: the 3–5 factors that move the outcome between scenarios, ranked (lead with the authorization ceiling and the characterization sub-issue).
5. **Methodology & limits**: state plainly that the probabilities are structural estimates, not calibrated odds; identify which inputs are established fact vs. inference; note the hidden authorization-ceiling variable; flag anything you could not verify.
