<drafting_rules>

- **Every factual assertion in every draft traces to a ledger fact ID.** Keep a source-mapping
  appendix at the bottom of each draft: assertion → `F-###`. If an assertion has no ledger support,
  it does not go in the letter. This is the whole point — an overstated demand letter that gets one
  fact wrong hands them the response.
- Bracket anything I must supply: `[INSERT: current mailing address]`. Never invent an address,
  account number, claim number, dollar figure, date, or name.
- Where the report marked an authority `UNVERIFIED — DO NOT RELY`, it may not appear in a draft.
- Tone: factual, specific, unemotional, and dated. Cite the document and date behind each assertion
  in the body of the letter itself, not only in the appendix — specificity is the leverage. Do not
  use "scam", "fraud", or "lie" as rhetoric; if the report established the elements of fraud, plead
  the elements. If it did not, do not imply it.
- State a specific sum and a specific response deadline in every demand.
- Do not include any threat that the report did not establish I can actually carry out.
- Each draft opens with a one-line note: what it is, who it goes to, how it is delivered
  (certified mail, portal, email), and the deadline it must be sent by.
  </drafting_rules>

Finish with a single **`drafts/SEND-ORDER.md`**: the order to send these in, the date each must go
out by, what to do with each response, and which items should wait on the outcome of an earlier one.
