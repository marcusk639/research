## 2. What makes people respond

These figures are vendor benchmarks, not peer-reviewed. No data specific to lawyers was found.

- **Base rates are low.** Cold email averages about 1.5–5% replies; targeted, personalized sends
  reach about 7.5% ([Woodpecker](https://woodpecker.co/blog/linkedin-vs-cold-email/),
  [Sopro](https://sopro.io/?p=42747)). LinkedIn outreach reports about 10%
  ([GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/cold-outreach-reply-rates-comparison)). Using both channels
  is reported to raise replies by about 50%
  ([Sproutworth](https://www.sproutworth.com/multichannel-cold-outreach/)).
- **Honoraria help.** About $100–150 is common for a 20–30 minute B2B session, and $150–200 an
  hour for senior professionals
  ([Koji](https://koji.so/docs/user-research-recruitment-email-templates),
  [Great Question](https://greatquestion.co/blog/customer-research-incentives-guide)). Incentives
  cut no-shows ([User Interviews](https://www.userinterviews.com/blog/pay-your-participants)). Offer
  a donation to a veterans charity as an alternative for people who can't take money, such as clinic
  faculty.
- **Inference, not measured:** naming the person's own talk ("your NOVA CUE series", "your AI
  workshop") should beat the generic rates above.
- **Professional responsibility.** The case is fictional, so there's no client information, and
  ABA Formal Opinion 512's confidentiality concerns don't arise
  ([ABA 512](https://lprb.mncourts.gov/Pages/Formal%20Opinion%20512%20-%20Generative%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Tools.pdf)).
  Ask for feedback, not an endorsement or testimonial.
