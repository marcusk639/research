# Phase 2 — Outline (v1, pre-retrieval)

Section → angles → what would settle it / what would falsify it.

1. **Executive summary & verdict** — all angles. Settled by: the income model + threat map + legality of the recommended model.
2. **Scope, method, assumptions** — manifest.
3. **The market as of Oct 2026** — C, F.
   - Settles: current fee-eligible accredited counts, VA fee pool, fee-agreement volume, CUE/effective-date frequency and dollar value (BVA CUE decision volume/grant rates; VA OIG effective-date error rates).
   - Falsifies: CUE motions are rare / rarely granted so the pain is episodic; accredited population has shrunk; fee pool falling.
4. **Customer pain & willingness to pay** — C, D.
   - Settles: how firms do this work today; adjacent spend (per-file services, seat tools); small-firm AI adoption rate and tech budgets.
   - Falsifies: practitioners report low interest in AI, low tech spend, or treat CUE as rare niche work.
5. **Threat map (ranked)** — A, B.
   - Settles: each competitor's current features (Oct 2026), pricing, funding, customer base; whether anyone ships effective-date/CUE reasoning.
   - Falsifies the wedge: any tool ships earliest-effective-date argumentation or CUE viability analysis; a legal-AI incumbent announces a VA vertical.
6. **Beat / route-around / partner, per threat** — A, B, D, F.
7. **Monetization models compared (with legality)** — D.
   - Settles: price benchmarks per model; legality of a non-accredited vendor selling per-file analysis to accredited reps; case-expense pass-through.
   - Falsifies: VA rules treat vendor-prepared analysis as unaccredited "preparation" of claims; firms can't pass costs through; API buyers don't exist.
8. **Moat status** — E.
   - Settles: whether commercial historical point-in-time CFR exists (granularity, API, price); BVA/CAVC corpus availability.
   - Falsifies the data moat: an affordable API-grade historical CFR exists.
9. **Distribution & CAC** — F.
   - Settles: NOVA sponsorship/exhibit costs, partner/integration channels, county/state VSO procurement practices.
10. **Durability & policy risk** — F.
    - Settles: GUARD VA / PLUS for Veterans Act status; VA automation/accuracy trends; portal access for attorneys/agents; ABA Op. 512 duties.
11. **Adjacent pivots** — G.
    - Settles: size, buyer, price tolerance, competition, and analog legal mechanism (reopening/onset-date/records-correction) for each candidate adjacent market.
    - Falsifies a pivot: crowded by funded AI players, or no retroactive-error mechanism worth automating.
12. **Income model** — synthesis (founder-facing modeling on verified inputs).
13. **Recommendation: model + niche + 90-day plan + kill criteria.**
14. **Limitations & open questions.**
15. **Bibliography + methodology appendix.**
