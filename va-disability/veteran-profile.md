## Critical Gaps — CONFIRM BEFORE STRATEGY (Phase 2 gate)

1. **Discharge characterization** (Honorable / General / OTH?). This gates basic
   eligibility — essential.
2. **STRs (Service Treatment Records):** does he have his service medical records, or
   need to request them? Do they document the ankle, insomnia, mental health?
3. **Current treatment / continuity:** is he in ongoing mental-health/SUD treatment
   now (needed to show current severity for ratings)?
4. **Tinnitus claim date / any hearing loss** alongside tinnitus.
5. **Any prior denied claims** beyond the granted tinnitus (affects supplemental vs.
   new-claim strategy and effective dates).
6. **Dependents** (spouse/children/parents) — affects compensation math (30%+).
7. **MST/harassment documentation:** were any reports filed, or is it lay-evidence
   only? Affects PTSD/mental-health stressor strategy.
8. **Employment detail for TDIU:** the pay cut + FMLA + PIP suggests possible TDIU
   relevance — need current income vs. "substantially gainful" threshold and whether
   he is/expects to be unable to maintain gainful employment.
