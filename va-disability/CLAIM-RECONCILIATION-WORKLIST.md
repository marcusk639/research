# Claim Reconciliation Worklist — for the Veteran + VSO + Clinician

> **Status:** active hand-off · **Created:** 2026-07-09 · **Type:** TYPE-B (claim-facing) reconciliation list — Phase 3 of [`PLAN-CORPUS-CONSISTENCY-REMEDIATION-2026-07-09.md`](./PLAN-CORPUS-CONSISTENCY-REMEDIATION-2026-07-09.md).
> **What this is:** every contradiction/inconsistency that touches a **signed statement, the actual VA record, a witness account, or the clinician's letter** — the ones that can only be resolved by the people who own that content. This is a _checklist to work through with your VSO_, not something the corpus can fix on its own.
> **Hard rule:** this document only **identifies** discrepancies. It contains **no drafted or suggested wording** for any statement. Each witness (Erica, Reyes, Poquette, Goff) resolves their own content in their **own words**; the clinician (Cherie) exercises her own judgment; the veteran's own statements are his own. Coached statements are the single biggest credibility risk in this claim — this list exists to _avoid_ that, not create it.
> **Not legal/medical advice.** Work these with an accredited VSO or VA-accredited attorney.

Each item: **the conflict** (neutral) · **why it matters** · **owner** · **the action**. Ordered most-consequential first.

## 1. B1 — "December 2024 job loss" (CRITICAL)

- **Conflict:** The veteran **confirms a December 2024 layoff that resulted from his service-connected conditions (SUD/PTSD)** — this is real. Corroborated by the late-2024 Starlite "recently laid off" readmission note (`COMPREHENSIVE-FINDINGS §2`). **But the date doesn't line up across the file:** the veteran's account and the Starlite record point to **late/Dec-2024**, while **Erica's signed statement lists job losses in 2018, 2023, and 2026 — no 2024 event.** Is Erica's "2023" the same job loss mis-dated, or a different job?
- **Why it matters:** presenting anticipated witness content as if the witness already provided it — on a claim with a documented coached-statement history — is the exact pattern that destroys credibility. It also risks a load-bearing "total occupational impairment" anchor resting on an attribution that isn't in the clean record.
- **Owner:** VSO + Veteran (confirm the event); Erica (her statement).
- **Action:** (1) **Veteran** attests to the Dec-2024 layoff and his experience of why, in his own statement (his own employment history — competent lay evidence). (2) **VSO** confirms the exact employment dates from records and that the Starlite "recently laid off" note is in the file. (3) **Cherie** provides the causation opinion (that the job loss stems from the SC conditions — the medical nexus). (4) **Erica** corroborates only if she genuinely recalls it, her own words. (5) **Reconcile the date** across the veteran's statement, Erica's statement (currently 2023), and the records so nothing conflicts. No document presents it as Erica's attribution unless she actually states it.

## 2. B5 / I-20 — Marriage characterization (HIGH)

- **Conflict:** the strategy relies on a **caretaker/roommate, ADL/hygiene-dependent** marriage. That framing appears in **no signed or authored document** — Erica's statement and the blog draft describe devotion and partnership; Cherie's letter mentions only "near-divorce strain."
- **Why it matters:** if the file later shows the caretaking dynamic in one place and a warm, reciprocal partnership in another, a rater sees a contradiction. Both can be true, but the file must read consistently — and the caregiving/ADL facts have to genuinely exist in the record, not just verbally.
- **Owner:** Veteran + Erica + Cherie.
- **Action:** each, in their own words/judgment, reflects the _genuine_ nature of the marriage and any real ADL assistance — consistently across Erica's statement, Cherie's notes, and anything else in the file. Keep the blog draft unpublished while the claim is open.

## 3. I-8 — Pre-service substance use vs. the signed statement (HIGH)

- **Conflict:** your signed personal statement reportedly states no pre-service substance use; treatment records carry self-reports of use around age 18.
- **Why it matters:** an apparent contradiction in a sworn statement is a credibility hit — but the underlying nuance (you used substances young without recognizing it _as_ a disorder until treatment named it) is legitimate and reconcilable.
- **Owner:** VSO + Veteran.
- **Action:** VSO confirms how this was previously "resolved" (a cover note? a supplemental statement?) and what's actually in the file. If needed, the Veteran files a corrective supplemental statement **in his own words** capturing the honest nuance — this is _his own_ statement (help with structure is allowed; content must be genuinely his), VSO/attorney to review before filing.

## 4. I-17 — Two conflicting "Recover" PsychEvals (HIGH)

- **Owner:** Veteran + Cherie + VSO.
- **Action:** do not submit either until reconciled; establish with Cherie/the vendor why two exist and confirm the operative causation; keep Cherie's in-service-MST/self-medication theory primary. Log the resolution in `DOCUMENT-PROVENANCE.md`.

## How to use this

Work top-down with your VSO. For every item, the fix is **truthful reconciliation by the person who owns the content** — never changing what a document says to make it more convenient. If a fact is genuinely unknown (the ankle date, the Dec-2024 job loss), find the truth in the records rather than choosing the helpful version. When Erica's and Cherie's updates exist, `claim-consistency-checker` can re-check the whole file for any remaining collisions.
