## Where this stands, honestly

Your rating is 10%, so entitlement runs **only** through **serious employment handicap**
(38 U.S.C. § 3102(a)(2)). The severity side is strong — six of the nine § 21.52(b) factors favor
you, and factor (8) names substance abuse expressly. **Causation is the weak link:** the law
requires the impairment trace in substantial part to a _service-connected_ disability, and yours
is tinnitus alone. The conditions that actually wrecked your work history are not
service-connected yet.

So a denial is a realistic outcome — and a recoverable one. Reapplying after the MH claim is
granted is routine. **What is not recoverable is a credibility problem.** That asymmetry should
drive every answer you give.
