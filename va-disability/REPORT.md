# VA Disability Ratings

> Status: active · Started: 2026-06-06 · Last updated: 2026-07-05

## Research Question

How should this veteran (USAF 2009–2013, F-15 Crew Chief, current SC tinnitus 10%,
confirmed PTSD/MST + GAD + MDD + OCD + SUD, currently unemployed) build and
sequence a multi-condition VA disability claim to reach a **100% schedular
rating without TDIU**, on realistic, truthfully-documented evidence?

## Key findings (2026-07-05 update)

- **Claim status:** a Supplemental Claim is open (VSO-filed ~5/19/2026); a VA
  development letter's soft deadline (~6/28/2026) has passed, but **no C&P exam
  has happened yet** — the file is still open for evidence.
- **Sleep apnea secondary to PTSD remains unfiled and unstarted:** it's the
  single highest-leverage secondary claim (see `100-PERCENT-SCHEDULAR-PATHWAY.md`
  §7 and `SLEEP-APNEA-SECONDARY-CLAIM.md`), but no sleep study has been completed —
  get a primary-care referral or private home sleep apnea test started this week;
  see `SLEEP-APNEA-SECONDARY-CLAIM.md` for why this is now the top blocking action.
- **The single most important open task:** no clinician has ever formally
  diagnosed PTSD anywhere in this record — it currently rests on the personal
  statement and buddy statements only. Getting a formal diagnosis from Cherie
  Mills matters more than the nexus opinion itself; see `CLAIM-ASSESSMENT.md`
  §0.3.
- **Realistic range:** 70% mental health is a confident floor; 80–90% combined is
  the realistic target; 100% schedular is a genuine but real stretch that depends
  on the C&P examiner finding "total occupational **and social** impairment,"
  not just inability to work.
- **The SUD theory changed, and GAD/MDD needs the same fix:** the June 2026
  iatrogenic in-service-opioid theory is contradicted by the veteran's own
  repeated clinical self-report across five Starlite admissions (2022–2025); the
  theory now led with is SUD as self-medication for the psychiatric conditions.
  The same records also show pre-service (age ~18) self-reported anxiety/
  depression onset, which means GAD/MDD is better argued as aggravation of a
  pre-existing condition or secondary to PTSD, not pure direct connection.
- **MST disclosure pattern:** the clinical record shows more denial than
  disclosure (2015 VA intake, 2018 Victory Recovery x2, 2023–25 Starlite x3) with
  one internally-contradicted partial disclosure in 2022 — the claim's real
  strength is the in-service disclosures to fellow service members, not a clean
  clinical paper trail; see `CLAIM-ASSESSMENT.md` §1.1 for the delayed-disclosure
  framing this requires.
- **Bright line, unchanged:** truthful documentation of genuine limitations is
  legitimate and supported; manufacturing or coaching evidence is fraud
  (38 U.S.C. § 6103; 18 U.S.C. § 1001) — see `CLAIM-ASSESSMENT.md` Part 0 for a
  specific, live example that needed correcting.
