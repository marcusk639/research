## Weaknesses

- The GAO citations are marked "UNVERIFIED (moderate)" in the table but the hedge isn't repeated where the claim is used in prose ("What's missing and the next step") — a reader of body text alone wouldn't see the same caveat.

## Gaps

- Recommends the nexus opinion be "modeled on the reasoning that succeeded in the two BVA decisions above" without addressing how this interacts with this corpus's own documented coaching-risk history (`CLAIM-ASSESSMENT.md` §0.2 treats a prior coached-statement episode as the single biggest credibility risk to the claim).
