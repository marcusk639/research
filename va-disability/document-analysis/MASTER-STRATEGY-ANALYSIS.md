## Summary

`MASTER-STRATEGY.md` is the designated "current working strategy doc" for this claim (per its own
header and `REPORT.md`'s "Start here" section). Its evidentiary purpose is not to serve as evidence
itself but to sequence action: what to fix before the pending C&P exam, how to frame the mental-health
cluster and SUD nexus theory, how to handle the ankle and secondary conditions, and how to treat TDIU
given the veteran's stated preference for a schedular-100% outcome. It explicitly builds on, and
defers to, `CLAIM-ASSESSMENT.md` (adversarial review) and `100-PERCENT-SCHEDULAR-PATHWAY.md` (legal
research) as companion documents.

## Score: 6/10 (accuracy · completeness-for-purpose · consistency · credibility — NOT persuasiveness)

**Driver: completeness-for-purpose, not accuracy.** Every specific factual claim in this document that
could be checked against `COMPREHENSIVE-FINDINGS-2026-07.md` and `CLAIM-ASSESSMENT.md` was confirmed,
not contradicted (see Consistency Check below) — accuracy is high. Internal consistency within the
document is also clean; no self-contradictions were found. The score is pulled down specifically
because, as the document meant to be "the one to work from going forward," it is missing four
consequential, already-known items from `COMPREHENSIVE-FINDINGS-2026-07.md` §0.8 and §9a — most
importantly the single item that source document calls its own **highest-priority finding**: that the
signed personal statement and the actual VA Form 21-0781 already assert, under penalty of perjury, the
opioid-origin theory this strategy document instructs the user to abandon (§1, action item 9). A
document whose stated job is "what to do about the file before the C&P exam" that omits its own source
material's top-ranked open item is a completeness problem with direct credibility-risk consequences,
not a wording or persuasiveness issue.

- The document correctly declines to draft or suggest wording for any buddy/spouse statement itself
  (e.g., line 72 says "finalize Erica's independent statement" without specifying content) — appropriately
  deferring to the individuals themselves, consistent with this analyst's own constraints.
