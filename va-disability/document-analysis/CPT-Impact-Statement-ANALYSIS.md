## Summary

"Impact Statement" worksheet, organized around the CPT protocol's five core belief
domains (Safety, Power/Control, Trust, Intimacy, Esteem) plus an opening "worst moment"
scene and the immediate emotional/cognitive/somatic reaction to it. Its stated purpose,
per its own header, is therapeutic — surfacing and eventually restructuring trauma-related
"stuck point" cognitions with Cherie Mills as part of ongoing CPT. It is not itself
VA-facing evidence, but per `DOCUMENT-PROVENANCE.md` and `COMPREHENSIVE-FINDINGS-2026-07.md`
§4, it is valuable underlying material that could inform Cherie Mills's clinical
impressions and, eventually, her nexus letter — which is why factual consistency with the
rest of the record matters even though this document itself is never submitted to VA.

  independently-recollected memory, it strengthens the case (drug-facilitated assault is
