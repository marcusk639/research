## Key rating rules that shape this case

- **Mental health is rated as ONE condition.** PTSD, depression, anxiety, and SUD are
  evaluated together under the General Rating Formula for Mental Disorders
  (38 CFR 4.130: 0/10/30/50/70/100%) — you do **not** stack them. The rating turns on
  **occupational and social impairment**, not the number of diagnoses.
  _(Framework standard; verify 4.130 wording — flagged in knowledge-base gaps.)_
- **No pyramiding** (38 CFR 4.14): insomnia symptoms fold into the mental-health rating
  rather than earning a separate one.
- **Tinnitus is capped at 10%** (already granted) — no upside there; pursue **hearing
  loss** separately only if an audiogram supports it. [knowledge-base §7, §3]

## Condition-by-condition plan

### 1. Mental health (PTSD + depression + anxiety + SUD) — the highest-value target

- **Route:** **Supplemental Claim** on the 2024 PTSD denial, expanded to claim
  depression/anxiety as part of the same psychiatric condition. The prior denial failed
  only because VA couldn't obtain the psychiatrist's evidence — **supply that evidence
  yourself** and the central defect is cured. [knowledge-base §5]
- **In-service nexus threads:** in-service insomnia treatment (Restoril/Ambien)
  corroborates onset; the hazing/harassment is the stressor (non-combat → needs credible
  lay support since no report was filed). [profile]
- **Severity argument:** 10+ inpatient rehab admissions, ongoing treatment, loss of a
  six-figure tech career, FMLA, PIP/severance → strong **occupational and social
  impairment** evidence pointing at the **50–70%** band.
- **Odds (REASONED ESTIMATE):**
  - _Service connection granted:_ **Moderate–High** — basis: evidentiary denial is
    fixable; insomnia Rx + continuity of care support nexus; weakness = no in-service MH
    records, non-combat stressor.
  - _Rating ≥ 70%:_ **Moderate** — basis: documented occupational collapse supports it,
    but VA may land at 50% absent strong DBQ language tying symptoms to "deficiencies in
    most areas."
- **Optimization levers:**
  1. Obtain **all private psychiatric + rehab records** (the exact evidence missing in
     2024). [knowledge-base §10]
  2. **Nexus letter** from a treating psychiatrist linking the condition to service and
     addressing the hazing stressor + in-service insomnia.
  3. **Completed DBQ** (38 CFR 4.130) by a treating provider using rating-criteria
     language (occupational/social impairment level).
  4. **Personal statement + buddy letters** establishing the in-service stressor and
     symptom timeline (substitutes for the unfiled harassment report).
  5. Frame SUD as **secondary to the mental-health condition and/or iatrogenic** (the
     in-service oxycodone script after the ankle injury) — not willful misconduct.

### 2. Ankle (in-service fracture) — strongest grant odds

- **Route:** new direct service-connection claim. [knowledge-base §4]
- **Odds (REASONED ESTIMATE):** _Granted:_ **High** — basis: documented in-service
  fracture + treatment, buddy letter from the friend who drove you to the hospital;
  contingent on retrieving STRs/hospital records. _Rating:_ likely **0–10%** (painful/
  limited motion) unless instability or significant limitation is shown.
- **Optimization levers:** request **STRs** and the off-base hospital records; buddy
  letter; current imaging/exam documenting residual pain and any limitation of motion.

### 3. Substance use disorder — leverage, mostly via #1

- **Route:** claim **secondary** to service-connected mental health and/or as
  **iatrogenic** from the in-service oxycodone prescription. Rated within the
  mental-health evaluation (not separately). [profile; knowledge-base §9]
- **Odds (REASONED ESTIMATE):** _As a severity driver for the MH rating:_ **Moderate–
  High**. _As an independent grant:_ **Low–Moderate** (nexus-dependent; SUD alone from
  misconduct is barred).
- **Lever:** the rehab records are powerful **severity** evidence for the MH rating —
  use them there.

### 4. Insomnia — fold in, don't file separately

- Subsumed into the mental-health rating (no pyramiding). Use the in-service Restoril/
  Ambien Rx as **nexus corroboration** for #1, not as its own claim. [knowledge-base §10]

### 5. Autism Spectrum Disorder — set expectations low

- **Odds (REASONED ESTIMATE):** _Direct/aggravation SC:_ **Low** — basis: ASD is
  developmental/pre-existing; service connection requires showing in-service
  **aggravation beyond natural progression**, which is hard. Don't anchor the strategy
  to it; mention only if a provider supports aggravation.

### 6. Hearing loss — quick check alongside tinnitus

- **Odds:** **Unknown** until an audiogram. Crew-chief flightline noise exposure makes it
  worth a screening; if measurable loss exists, it's a separate ratable condition.

## TDIU — not now, but track it

You currently earn ~$157K → **substantially gainful employment**, so TDIU is **off the
table today**. [knowledge-base §6] It becomes relevant **only if** your
service-connected mental health later renders you unable to maintain substantially
gainful employment (marginal/odd jobs excepted). If the job loss leads to inability to
work — not a voluntary lower-paying choice — and you hold ≥70% combined with a 40%+
anchor, TDIU could pay at the 100% rate. **Document any work impairment now** so the
record exists if that day comes.
