## Global Constraints (apply to every task — copied from the report's ethics guardrails)

- **Never coach witness evidence.** For any veteran or witness statement, this
  plan names only the _topic_ to address and the _fact_ to reconcile. The
  declarant writes the content in their **own unedited words**. No task in this
  plan drafts, paraphrases, or "suggests language" for a statement — doing so is
  a plan failure. (38 U.S.C. § 6103; 18 U.S.C. § 1001; report guardrails.)
- **Substance only.** Clerical defects (DOB typo, Kline/Klein, phone
  transposition, apartment B504/B508, OCR artifacts) are **not** remediated as
  substantive items — they carry no rating weight. Fix them silently if a
  document is being re-executed for another reason; never file solely to correct
  one.
- **The objective is the maximum rating the genuine conditions legitimately
  support** — never fabricate, exaggerate, or misrepresent. Several tasks here
  _reconcile the veteran's own record against itself_; the reconciliation must be
  truthful, not strategically shaded.
- **Route corrective sworn statements through the accredited representative.**
  Amending a signed, certified statement (21-0781 / personal statement) carries
  its own credibility and strategic risk — see Task 8's caution. The decision to
  file, and how, belongs to the veteran + VA-accredited attorney, not to this
  plan.
- **Source-of-truth rule:** the co-located originals in
  `va-disability/original-records/` control over any markdown transcription; any
  load-bearing date/dose/diagnosis pulled from an OCR'd file must be verified
  against the source page image before it is relied on or rebutted.

## Filing & submission checklist — getting each item in front of the rater

> **Core principle:** the rater decides only from the official **claims file** (the
> C-file in VBMS). This plan, the rating report, and every analysis doc in
> `va-disability/` are **internal — the rater never sees them.** Each fact and
> record below only "counts" once it is filed through a VA channel and **associated
> with the claim.** Filing is the step that converts developed evidence into rater-
> visible evidence; skipping it wastes everything upstream.

**Step A — Fix the claim posture first (determines the vehicle for everything below).**

- [ ] REP confirms whether the claim is **open/in development** or **already decided**:
  - **Open** → file each item below as evidence _into the open claim_ (it will be
    decided with this evidence).
  - **Decided** → the vehicle changes: file a **Supplemental Claim (VA Form 20-0995)**
    with these as "new and relevant" evidence, or **Higher-Level Review (VA Form
    20-0996)** / Board appeal if no new evidence is being added (rare here — most of
    this _is_ new evidence, so 20-0995 is the likely path).

**Step B — Submission path (same three options for every item).**

      _associated with the claim_ in VBMS (an upload that isn't linked to the claim
