# Do This Week — VA Claim Kickoff

> One page. Pulled from [`CLAIM-EXECUTION-PLAN.md`](./CLAIM-EXECUTION-PLAN.md) Phase 1 + Phase 2.
> Goal this week: **lock the effective date, open free care, and start the record pulls.** Not legal advice.

## The one thing that can't wait

- [ ] **File VA Form 21-0966 (Intent to File) on VA.gov — today.** It backdates every condition you file within 12 months. Save the confirmation number. _Every day's delay = lost backpay._

## Free care (start the treatment record)

- [ ] **Call the Austin Vet Center — 512-416-1314** (1524 S. IH-35, Ste 100). Book MST counseling intake under 38 U.S.C. § 1720D (no copay, no enrollment barrier; records stay confidential from VBA until you authorize release).
- [ ] **Submit VA Form 10-10EZ** to enroll at CTVHCS (Austin VA Clinic, 7901 Metropolis Dr). Ask for the **MST Coordinator** at intake.

## Records to request (all free — send the requests, don't wait for replies)

- [ ] **SF-180 to NPRC** — full OMPF + service treatment records, 2009–2013.
- [ ] **FOIA to Air Force Safety Center + 4th Fighter Wing** — IH surveys, HAZMAT logs, SDS for JP-8, Cr(VI) corrosion products, hydraulic fluid, jet oil.
- [ ] **EPA SEMS record** for Seymour Johnson AFB (EPA ID NC7570024474).
- [ ] **Texas PDMP** opioid history (TX State Board of Pharmacy patient portal).
- [ ] **SSA earnings record** (Form SSA-7050).
- [ ] **Both outpatient therapists** who received the MST disclosure — request complete records.

## Ankle (your C&P is already done)

- [ ] **Request your ankle C&P/DBQ exam report** from VA.gov. Confirm it recorded ROM **in degrees**, in **4 positions** (active/passive, weight-bearing/non), noted **pain on motion** and the **cracking**. If not → ask VA for **weight-bearing ankle X-rays** (arthritis path).

## Get help (free)

- [ ] **Schedule a VA-accredited attorney consult** (no fee until after the first decision) — Texas Veterans Commission legal services or the NOVA directory.

## Two hard rules this week

- [ ] **Do NOT file TDIU** or claim unemployability (income too high — it backfires).
- [ ] **Buddy letters: witnessed facts only.** True ones help enormously; coached ones are a federal crime and sink the MST claim. Don't solicit anything anyone didn't actually see.

## Don't do yet (later phases)

- ✗ Don't file the 21-526EZ (wait for nexus letters + sleep study).
- ✗ Don't release SUD records to VBA (review with counsel first).
- ✗ Don't enroll in the Burn-Pit Registry (you don't qualify; signals a false theory).
