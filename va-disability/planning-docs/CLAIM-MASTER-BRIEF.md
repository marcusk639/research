## 1. The whole game, in one paragraph

Two convergent routes to 100%: **Route 1** — schedular 100% on the mental-health cluster alone (requires _total occupational **and** social_ impairment). **Route 2** — 90% MH + a 50% sleep-apnea secondary = 100% combined. Pursue **both**; Route 2 is **modestly higher-probability than Route 1 IF the sleep study is positive; it inherits the full 90%-MH burden and is NOT a safe fallback** — treat it as co-equal, not a superior path. Honest odds (REASONED ESTIMATES): 70% ≈ 90%+, 90% ≈ 60–70%, 100% MH-alone ≈ 40–50%, 100% combined ≈ 50–60%. **If you do only four things:** (1) get the sleep study, (2) get the ADL/hygiene dependence + the two delusions into Cherie's chart **and** DBQ before any C&P, (3) have Erica revise her own statement to document isolation _outside_ the marriage, (4) ask the VSO the effective-date/prior-decision question this week (free to ask, changes the back-pay stakes).

## 4. What to do with the DBQ for this supplemental claim

- **Correct form: VA Form 21-0960P-2 (Mental Disorders).** A private treating provider (Cherie) **can** complete and submit it. **She legally cannot** complete the initial-PTSD DBQ (21-0960P-4) — that is VHA/contract-examiner-only — so PTSD-specific narrative rides in her _letter_, not the form. [Full form-family explainer: `CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` §2.2.]
- **Attach a short narrative addendum** (or fold it into her updated letter): tie each checked symptom to its § 4.130 impairment tier and to concrete functional consequences. Bare DBQs get discounted because the form doesn't require the clinician to explain _why_ a box is checked — and Vazquez-Claudio requires symptoms be shown to _cause_ the impairment. This is the single most important DBQ move.
- **§1C** is the single-select occupational/social-impairment checkbox the rater leans on hardest; **§7 "Remarks"** (optional) is where DASS-21/WHODAS scores go _if she chooses_ to reference them.
- **Timing:** get it done and into the file **before** any C&P exam; submit via Cherie directly, or through you/the VSO.

## 6. Guardrails (non-negotiable)

- **Never coach witness evidence.** No drafting, scripting, or example wording for Erica's or any buddy statement — ever, however phrased. Identify gaps as open questions; the witness answers from their own memory. This is the single biggest documented credibility risk in this claim (`CLAIM-ASSESSMENT.md` §0).

- **Every legal citation gets `citation-verifier` before it enters a filed brief** — 40 of 109 citations were misstated in one prior pass. New cites in the 2026-07-08 docs are research-grade and not yet run through that pass.
