## 1. Bottom line up front

**The realistic default outcome is 70–90%. 100% by a schedular route is a genuine, earned possibility — not the expected result — and it hinges on two hard contingencies that are not yet resolved.**

**Honest odds — REASONED ESTIMATES, not statistically derived** (verbatim from `PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §9 / `CLAIM-MASTER-BRIEF.md` §1; these are the honesty anchor — nothing in this document may drift above them):

- **70% mental health ≈ 90%+**
- **90% mental health ≈ 60–70%**
- **100% schedular, MH-alone (Route 1) ≈ 40–50%**
- **100% schedular, combined (Route 2) ≈ 50–60%**

**The two hard contingencies that pin these down — read these first:**

1. **Route 1's married-veteran / total-social-impairment problem (the doctrinal linchpin).** Reaching 100% on the mental-health cluster alone requires "total occupational **AND** social impairment" (38 CFR 4.130 [pending citation-verifier — text confirmed in `100-PERCENT-SCHEDULAR-PATHWAY.md` §1]), and you are married with a spouse who actively helps with your care. The persuasive Board authority on whether a maintained marriage defeats "total social impairment" **cuts against you on the current count: ≈3 adverse vs. 1 favorable** (`CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` §3 — adverse: BVA 1513531, 22009295, 22015499; favorable: BVA 1648197; all non-precedential, none yet citation-verified). No decision adopts the corpus's "caregiving-dependency" framing. This is **supportable but not settled**, turns entirely on the factual characterization of the marriage, and is the single biggest reason Route 1's odds do not go higher (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §2.2 / I-3).

2. **Route 2's not-yet-obtained sleep study.** The combined route depends on a 50% sleep-apnea secondary, and **no polysomnography-confirmed OSA diagnosis exists.** A current diagnosis is a hard prerequisite under 38 CFR 3.310 [pending citation-verifier — `SLEEP-APNEA-SECONDARY-CLAIM.md` "Bottom line" / rating section]; a C&P exam does not generate a sleep-study referral on its own. Until the study exists, Route 2 is a theory, not a filable claim (`SLEEP-APNEA-SECONDARY-CLAIM.md` "What's missing").

**Operational state that makes all of this actionable (confirmed 2026-07-07):** the Supplemental Claim on the mental-health cluster was acknowledged by VA **29 May 2026**, **no C&P exam is scheduled yet**, and the VSO is submitting records as they are sent — **the evidence window is open** (`CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` §1; `PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` I-15). Everything below that must "land before the exam" still can. The moment a C&P is scheduled is the hard cutoff.

## 2. The two convergent schedular routes

Both routes share ~90% of the same evidence work, so they run in parallel rather than as a bet on one. **The center of gravity of both is identical: maximize the single mental-health rating.** From a 70% MH anchor the realistic combined ceiling is ~90% even with a full secondary stack; from a 90% MH anchor, one 50% sleep-apnea rating cleanly reaches 100%. Raising MH from 70→90 is worth more than any pile of marginal secondaries (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §1; combined-rating math in `100-PERCENT-SCHEDULAR-PATHWAY.md` §7).

### Route 1 — Schedular 100% on the mental-health cluster (the ideal)

The rater finds **total occupational AND social impairment** under 38 CFR 4.130, built from genuine symptoms across all five diagnoses that feed the single MH score (PTSD/MST + MDD + GAD + OCD + SUD, rated as one under anti-pyramiding, 38 CFR 4.14 [pending citation-verifier]). Architecture (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §4):

- **Two 100%-tier anchors carry the "total" argument** — both genuine, both **not yet in any treatment record**, which is exactly the work to be done:
  - **ADL / personal-hygiene dependence** (spouse-assisted) — matches the 100%-tier "intermittent inability to perform activities of daily living (including maintenance of minimal personal hygiene)." First 100%-tier-language symptom anywhere in the ~600-page corpus (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §3 / `CLAIM-ASSESSMENT.md` Part 2). Now first-corroborated objectively by the WHODAS 2.0 self-care domain (62.5%), with self-report caveats.
  - **Two persistent delusions (persecutory + grandiose)** — explicit 100%-tier "persistent delusions" language. Grandiose is already Cherie's own clinical assessment; neither is yet in her signed letter or any record (`COMPREHENSIVE-FINDINGS-2026-07.md` §0.14). Diagnostically distinct from PTSD/GAD/MDD/OCD — Cherie's clinical call whether this is "PTSD with psychotic features" or a separate psychotic-spectrum picture; rated under the same 4.130 formula either way.
- **The mandatory social prong** is supplied by concrete, near-total isolation _outside_ the marriage — no friends, no voluntary social contact, confinement to home — with the marriage characterized honestly as a **caregiving dependency, not reciprocal social function** (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §2.2 / I-3). WHODAS social domains (getting-along 70%, participation 75%, "making new friends = cannot do") support _severe_ — not by themselves _total_ — social impairment.
- **A dense 70%-tier floor** proves pervasiveness: near-continuous depression/anxiety (DASS-21 all three subscales "Extremely severe," 98–100th %ile), OCD obsessional rituals (a textual 70%-tier symptom), daily passive SI described honestly at the 70%-tier line (not dressed up as the 100%-tier "persistent danger"). (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §4.1/§4.3.)
- **Legal leverage that de-risks a lowball examiner:** the rater weighs the whole record, not just the exam snapshot (38 CFR 4.126(a) [pending citation-verifier — text quoted in `CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` §2.3]); a treating clinician's opinion/DBQ endorsing "total impairment" can outweigh a skeptical C&P examiner. Cherie's signed letter already states, in her own words, "total impairment in occupational and social realms" (`MASTER-STRATEGY.md` §Where-things-stand; `PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §2.1).

### Route 2 — Schedular 100% combined (the insurance, and co-equal target)

**90% MH + 50% sleep apnea = 95 → rounds to 100%** (38 CFR 4.25 whole-person math [pending citation-verifier — `100-PERCENT-SCHEDULAR-PATHWAY.md` §7 Scenario C]). Grounded in `SLEEP-APNEA-SECONDARY-CLAIM.md`:

- **The theory is sound but not yet filable.** Secondary connection runs through 38 CFR 3.310; the strongest causal pathway is PTSD hyperarousal lowering the arousal threshold, with two real (non-precedential) BVA grants on this exact fact pattern that succeeded on **treating-provider** opinions citing peer-reviewed literature, not paid IMO firms (`SLEEP-APNEA-SECONDARY-CLAIM.md` "legal framework"; BVA 1648003, A21018009 — non-precedential [pending citation-verifier]).
- **The 50% CPAP tier is confirmed** (38 CFR 4.97 DC 6847 [pending citation-verifier — verified verbatim in `SLEEP-APNEA-SECONDARY-CLAIM.md` citation log #1]) but sits under a pending-rule (NPRM) risk: a proposed rule would restructure DC 6847 so the automatic 50%-for-CPAP tier disappears (proposed tiers 0/10/50/100 — full CPAP control could fall to 0–10%, a near-total zero-out, not a drop to 30%). Still proposed, not final; current 50% tier remains in effect; existing ratings expected to be grandfathered. **Implication: file promptly once diagnosed** so a 50% grant lands and grandfathers before any rule change (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §5.2; `SLEEP-APNEA-SECONDARY-CLAIM.md` rating note).
- **Only three secondaries genuinely move the math:** sleep apnea (50%, load-bearing linchpin), migraines (30%, only if genuinely prostrating), ankle (10%). ED is worth claiming for SMC-K money but adds 0% to the percentage; GERD/hypertension/hearing-loss/gait-secondary joints are low-value or speculative — do not build on them (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §5.1).

**Why both at once:** arguing for 100% (Route 1) does not forfeit 90% (Route 2's anchor) — the rater assigns whatever the evidence supports. Build the record to genuinely support "total," present it that way, and let 90%+sleep-apnea be the floor you land on if the examiner stops short. The sleep study is what makes that floor a **100%** floor rather than a **90%** one (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` §6).

### TDIU — the labeled, rejectable fallback (not pursued)

TDIU is **explicitly declined** — the veteran wants to retain earning capacity and reach 100% through the schedular criteria themselves. It cannot be fully suppressed: under _Rice v. Shinseki_ [pending citation-verifier] VA must consider TDIU once unemployability is raised by the record, but this is low-risk — worst case VA grants it as an automatic fallback floor (paying at the 100% rate) while the schedular-100% argument continues. To keep schedular-100% alive, frame everything as **total occupational AND social impairment**, never "can't work" alone (`MASTER-STRATEGY.md` §4; `100-PERCENT-SCHEDULAR-PATHWAY.md` §6).

## 3. Do this next, in order (the highest-leverage spine)

Ordered by leverage; items 1–3 are the whole game and must land **before the C&P exam is scheduled.** Each traces to its corpus source. **None of these involves coaching a witness or reaching for a symptom** — they are timing and completeness for impairment that is already real.

1. **The sleep study — single highest-leverage action.** Book a private home sleep apnea test (faster, ~1–3 weeks, legally acceptable as the current-diagnosis element) or a VA PCP referral, reporting snoring / witnessed apneas / daytime fatigue explicitly. This is the one action that converts a 90% MH rating into a 100% combined rating. (`SLEEP-APNEA-SECONDARY-CLAIM.md` "next step"; `PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` Phase 1 / I-12; `CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` Tier 1 #1.)

2. **Get the two genuine 100%-tier symptoms into Cherie's treatment record — as a care matter first.** Raise the ADL/hygiene dependence and both delusions with Cherie now, so she can assess, chart, and decide the diagnostic characterization in her own clinical judgment. Then ask whether she will provide an **updated letter + a Mental Disorders DBQ (VA Form 21-0960P-2)** — the legally correct form, since PTSD has never been service-connected so she cannot complete the initial-PTSD DBQ (21-0960P-4, VHA/contract-examiner only) — with a **short narrative addendum tying each checked symptom to its 4.130 impairment tier and concrete functional consequences** (bare DBQs get discounted; this also satisfies _Vazquez-Claudio_). Do not tell her what to conclude — she has already independently concluded "total impairment." (`CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` §2.2 / Tier 1 #3 / Tier 2 #6; `PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` I-1(b) / I-5; `CLAIM-MASTER-BRIEF.md` §3–4.)

3. **Erica's independent statement — her own words only, documenting isolation _outside_ the marriage.** It is the primary written channel for the ADL/hygiene fact and the social prong, and it is load-bearing precisely because the married-veteran authority runs 3-to-1 against — concrete isolation everywhere outside the marriage (no friends, no extended-family contact, no community) is the strongest rebuttal. **Hard constraint (law + this corpus's #1 rule): do not draft, script, or supply wording — a coached statement is the single biggest credibility risk in this claim** (`CLAIM-ASSESSMENT.md` §0; `COMPREHENSIVE-FINDINGS-2026-07.md` §0.2). Log provenance once it exists. (`CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` §3 / Tier 2 #8; `PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` I-6.)

4. **Establish and attend the new VA mental-health provider** (WellMind / PSO referral) so current symptoms are contemporaneously charted by a second source before the exam. (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` Phase 2; `CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` Tier 2 #7.)

5. **Send the objective instruments now, honestly framed.** DASS-21 (09 Jun 2026) and WHODAS 2.0 (11 Jun 2026) to the VSO with a cover note stating plainly they are self-administered, vendor-auto-scored instruments — real evidence of severity/function, not a stand-alone rating basis. The honest framing is a credibility asset. (`CLAIM-MASTER-BRIEF.md` §2 item 1; `PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` I-14.)

6. **Ask the VSO the effective-date / prior-decision question this week.** Confirm (a) the date and outcome of any prior decision on the mental-health issue and (b) whether this is legally a Supplemental Claim reopening it or the first adjudication — this governs whether 38 CFR 3.2500(h)(1)'s potential retroactive effective date applies or (h)(2)'s cap [pending citation-verifier]. Free to ask; materially changes the back-pay stakes. (`CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` §2.5 / Tier 0.)

7. **Prepare, in writing, the two honest explanations** the record will otherwise raise cold: (a) why the records are silent on the withheld symptoms — the veteran's own account of deliberate minimization in addiction treatment to avoid involuntary holds and before he trusted providers, corroborable clinically by Cherie; and (b) the SI-fluctuation and trauma-disclosure patterns. In the veteran's own words. (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` I-2(d) / I-7 / I-9; `CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` Tier 2 #9–10.)

8. **Confirm the DC 6847 NPRM's current status** before relying on the 50% CPAP tier, and **ask the VSO to flag the instant a C&P is scheduled** (the documentary cutoff). (`CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` Tier 1 #4–5.)

**Then, at the C&P exam** (work from `CP-EXAM-PREP-MENTAL-HEALTH.md`): argue **both** prongs, never occupation-only; describe worst/typical days, not best days; cover the ADL/hygiene dependence fully and the delusions (already charted via step 2, so not surfacing cold); SI honestly at the 70%-tier line; total isolation concretely. Expect an in-person/telehealth exam regardless of how strong the private evidence is; if the examiner is dismissive of Cherie's findings, that is not decisive — the rater weighs the whole record (`CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` Tier 4).

**After the decision:** if MH lands at 90% and the sleep study is positive → pursue the sleep-apnea secondary to reach 100% combined. If the decision fails to engage with Cherie's opinion → challenge in writing on exam-adequacy / whole-record grounds before the one-year deadline (38 CFR 3.2500 [pending citation-verifier]), calendared the moment any decision issues. (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` Phase 6; `CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` Tier 5.)

## 5. Realism check — where the corpus reads as too optimistic

Flagged for the orchestrator to hold the line on; none of these should push any number above the §1 anchor.

- **Route 1 (40–50%) is the corpus's most fragile number and should not drift up.** It rests on three symptoms that are _not yet in any record_ (the two delusions and ADL dependence) plus a social prong facing 3-to-1 adverse authority. If steps 2–3 don't land before the exam, revise Route 1 **down**, not merely hold it. `CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` §3 already says the 3-to-1 adverse count is a reason _not_ to let 40–50% drift upward — preserve that discipline.
- **"Cherie's letter says total impairment" is not yet a settled odds-improver.** `MASTER-STRATEGY.md` §Where-things-stand explicitly cautions the letter carries an internal tension (the "total impairment" sentence sits next to "obsessive overworking") and a now-resolved-but-formerly-open credibility question — treat it as helpful evidence a rater weighs, not a done deal.
- **The objective instruments (DASS-21, WHODAS) are self-report vendor data, not examiner findings**, taken around the June-2026 relapse period. They corroborate _severe_ impairment and firm the 70%-tier floor / 90% estimate; they do **not** by themselves prove the _total_ impairment the 100% tier requires (`PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` I-14 / §11). Do not let them be read as decisive.
- **Route 2's "clean 100%" assumes three things not yet true at once:** MH actually reaching 90%, a positive sleep study, and the 50% CPAP tier surviving the NPRM. It is more reliable than Route 1 but is still conditioned — the 50–60% already reflects that; don't round it up to "the safe path."
- **The new legal leverage (Nieves-Rodriguez, §4.2, the two added adverse BVA cites) is research-grade and unverified.** `CLAIM-COMPLETION-PLAN-2026-07-08.md` §6 explicitly flags these for citation-verifier before any filing. Do not present them as settled in the master guide.
