# VA Submission Guide — What to Send, What to Hold, What to Never Submit

> **Status:** active · **Created:** 2026-07-08
> **Purpose:** a single, document-by-document answer to "what do I actually send to the VA" — built from `DOCUMENT-PROVENANCE.md`'s authoritative status log (the only place in this corpus that tracks signed/submitted status per document), cross-checked against the open action items in `PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md` and `ACTION-CHECKLIST-100-PERCENT.md`. This doesn't replace either — it answers a narrower, very concrete question those documents don't organize around: _of everything in this corpus, what actually goes to VA, what's already there, and what should never leave this folder._
> **Not legal advice.** Confirm every submission decision with your VSO before sending — especially the "already submitted?" status calls below, which I inferred from signature dates and the "VSO submitting records as sent" pattern in `REPORT.md`, not from a direct confirmation of VA's claims-file contents.

---

## 1. How submissions actually reach the file

The working channel is **your VSO** — per `REPORT.md`'s 2026-07-07 update, they are "actively submitting records as you send them," and the evidence window stays open until a C&P exam is scheduled. Feed items as they're ready rather than waiting to bundle everything into one package (I-15 in `PATH-TO-100-PERCENT-2026-07-07.md`) — each item strengthens the file the moment it lands. Mail/AccessVA QuickSubmit and the specific VA form for each document type are listed in §7 below if you ever need to submit directly rather than through the VSO.
