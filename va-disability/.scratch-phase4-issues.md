### I-3 ★★★ — Two-domain requirement: "total occupational AND social impairment" vs. the marriage (the doctrinal linchpin; adverse authority now ~3-to-1)

- **Severity:** Critical.
- **Why it threatens 100%:** 38 CFR 4.130 requires **both** total occupational **and** total social impairment for 100%, and 4.126(b) bars a rating "solely on the basis of social impairment" [confirmed, PATH-TO-100 §11] — so the case needs both prongs, documented. The veteran is **married and his spouse actively helps with his care**, and a rater can argue a maintained marriage cuts against _total social_ impairment. Persuasive Board authority now runs **~3 adverse (BVA 22015499, 1513531, 22009295) vs. 1 favorable (BVA 1648197)** [all non-precedential; all pending citation-verifier], and **no decision adopts the "caregiving-dependency" wording** — so this is supportable but unsettled and turns entirely on the factual characterization of the marriage.
- **Solutions:**
  - **Veteran + Cherie + Erica:** frame the marriage honestly as a **caregiving dependency, not reciprocal social function** — Erica's care-assistance is a _severity_ marker (a 100%-tier ADL signal), and the veteran is otherwise isolated. Cherie and Erica each describe the relationship in caregiving terms _in their own words_, truthfully.
  - **Erica (load-bearing per Phase-3 finding):** document **concrete isolation _outside_ the marriage** — no friends, no extended-family contact, no community participation, confinement to home (the 1451194/1623106 pattern) — because the adverse authority specifically defeats claims where _some_ relationship is shown. Her words only.
  - **Attorney (post-decision, if reached):** anchor argument on BVA 1648197 and be ready to distinguish 22015499/1513531/22009295 [pending citation-verifier] as persuasive positioning, not controlling law.
