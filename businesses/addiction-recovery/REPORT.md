# Addiction Recovery — Where AI + This Founder's Profile Wins

> Status: draft · Started: 2026-06-19 · Evidence base: deep-research pass `wf_ef58675b-672` (104 agents, ~4.96M tokens, 22 sources, 25 claims adversarially verified → 24 confirmed / 1 killed). Confidence labels: `[Verified]` (2–3 vote) · `[Inference]` · `[Estimate]` · `[Time-sensitive]`.

## Research Question

Across the addiction-recovery field, where does AI/LLM software give **this specific solo founder** (deep AI/LLM specialty; ~10 yrs senior SWE; USAF honorably-discharged, service-connected **disabled veteran**; personal recovery history) a _particularly strong, durable_ competitive advantage — or a uniquely strong path to market entry and revenue — building on the existing `recovery-platform` assets (Homegroups, RATS/regroup, NextStep, recovery-api) and the prior conclusion that recovery _money flows to operators and billable human services, never to the recovering member_?

## Key Findings (one-paragraph answer)

**The AI edge alone is necessary but not sufficient, and not defensible by itself. The two "obvious" software wedges — selling AI documentation/RCM tooling to SUD operators, and SUD ambient-scribe — are already contested by funded, category-native incumbents and should not be entered head-on.** The single _structural_, founder-specific moat the evidence surfaces is the **SDVOSB federal-procurement channel**: VA "Veterans First" contracting is a **mandatory, codified preference** (Kingdomware "Rule of Two," SDVOSB-first order of priority, sole-source up to $5M, a 5% government-wide goal) that a solo service-connected-disabled-veteran owner satisfies trivially and that virtually no AI competitor can match. **The decisive move is therefore the channel, not the algorithm: become a certified SDVOSB and sell competent AI back-office / operational tooling into VA/VHA and veteran-serving SUD programs (and, in parallel, to private sober-living _operators_ below the EMR tier), while keeping every dollar of monetization clear of EKRA's referral-remuneration wire.** `[Inference, high]`

---

## Problem & Customer

The buyers who actually pay in this field are **operators and agencies**, not people in recovery (confirmed prior conclusion, unchallenged by this pass):

- **SUD treatment operators** (residential / PHP / IOP / outpatient) and **sober-living operators** — they pay for anything that protects reimbursement, reduces documentation labor, or survives an audit.
- **The VA / VHA and veteran-serving SUD programs** — a government buyer with a _legally mandated_ preference to route work to disabled-veteran-owned firms.
- **Providers drowning in payer AI** — clinicians who increasingly face automated, batch insurance denials and want oversight/appeal tooling.

The recovering member remains a poor direct customer: community-recovery features churn and face 12-step Tradition backlash. Nothing in this pass changes that. `[Verified prior]`

## Market (and how the numbers were derived)

- **Ambient clinical documentation** is healthcare AI's first breakout category — **~$600M in 2025 (+2.4x YoY)**, but _concentrating fast_: Nuance DAX Copilot ~33%, Abridge ~30%, Ambience ~13%, with Abridge+Ambience taking ~70% of _new_ share. `[Estimate — single VC source (Menlo Ventures 2025), share-concentration corroborated by Becker's]` → A solo builder cannot out-resource these on the general-scribe axis.
- **AI prior-authorization**: "$100M+ market, ~10x YoY" (≈$10M 2024 → $100M 2025). Real and fast, but a **small-number artifact** off a tiny base relative to the ~$98B admin-spend TAM. `[Estimate — single VC source, growth direction corroborated by healthcarehuddle/NAIC]`
- **AI coding/billing (RCM)**: ~$450M (2025); documentation + back-office RCM ≈60% of health-IT spend (~$38B). `[Estimate — single VC source; weight as directional, not bankable]`
- **Veteran procurement (the real lever)**: a **government-wide 5% SDVOSB goal** (raised from 3% by the FY2024 NDAA), plus VA-specific **sole-source up to $5M**. This is not a TAM estimate — it is a _codified preference_ that shrinks the effective competitor set rather than enlarging the pie. `[Verified]`

Net: the open-market AI TAMs are real but modest and crowded; the _procurement preference_ is the asset that's actually scarce and matched to this founder.

## Business Model — the ranked wedges

**#1 — Veteran-procurement channel + AI SUD/behavioral-health operational tooling (the moat). `[Inference, high]`**
Become a **certified SDVOSB** (SBA VetCert) and sell AI-enabled operational/back-office tooling into **VA/VHA SUD programs and veteran-serving providers**, where the **Rule of Two is mandatory** (Kingdomware, 2016) and **SDVOSB sits first in the order of priority** (VAAR 819.70, 38 U.S.C. 8127(h)), with **sole-source up to $5M**. The defensibility is the _channel_, not the model — competitors can copy the AI; they cannot copy the disabled-veteran ownership test (13 CFR 128.202) the founder passes trivially. Revenue = government contracts (flat-fee software/services), the cleanest EKRA posture available.

**#2 — Sober-living-operator AI tooling below the EMR tier (the RATS buyer). `[Inference, med]`**
The one open-market crack: operator-side census/compliance/alumni-outcomes AI for sober living, where RATS/regroup already exists in the codebase (operators are the buyer that pays) and the EMR incumbents don't reach. Best as the _commercial_ complement to the VA channel and a warm reference base. Highest codebase reuse.

**#3 — Provider-side denials/appeals/UR support AI. `[Estimate]`**
Real, measured demand; but a contested RCM lane and a longer build. Viable only as a _narrow SUD/veteran-scoped_ feature riding the #1 channel, not as a standalone open-market play.

**#4 — "Picks-and-shovels" engagement-data/infra licensed to a reimbursement-backed CM/peer provider. `[Inference, low-med]`**
The Homegroups opt-in data layer the prior research flagged — viable _only_ as a **flat-fee software license** to the billable party, never per-referral, and only after a Schena-style undue-influence review. Upside, not a plan.

**Reject — SUD ambient-scribe as a standalone, and consumer-side recovery AI. `[Verified]`** Contested/concentrating market and Tradition-churn respectively.

## Risks & Unknowns

- **EKRA is a live federal _criminal_ wire** (18 U.S.C. 220): all-payer (covers commercial insurance), up to 20 yrs / $200K per violation, and **now reaches third-party marketing intermediaries** (_US v. Schena_, 9th Cir., July 2025 — first appellate ruling; payment need not flow to a provider to violate). Every wedge must sell **software/tooling for a flat fee**, never take remuneration tied to steering patients to treatment/facilities. `[Verified][Time-sensitive — Schena binds only the 9th Circuit; 2025–26 EKRA updates in flux]`
- **Incumbent depth is partly unverified vendor marketing** — Ritten/Alleva AI _exist_ (product pages confirm), but the _quality/SUD-specificity_ of those features is unproven; that's the only crack for a deeper SUD-compliance build. `[Caveat]`
- **Headline TAMs are single-source (Menlo 2025) VC estimates** with boosterism risk — directional, not bankable. `[Estimate]`
- **Procurement is gated on certification**: SDVOSB sole-source eligibility requires _completed_ SBA VetCert (post-2023 transfer from VA CVE) before any contract revenue. `[Verified][Time-sensitive]`
- **Open questions that gate the #1 call**: (a) Are there _actual_ active/forecast VA/VHA solicitations for SUD/behavioral-health operational software where Rule-of-Two would trigger an SDVOSB set-aside — i.e., is the demand real or theoretical for this exact category? (b) Realistic time-to-first-revenue and sales-cycle length: VA via VetCert/sole-source **vs** private sober-living operators direct — and how much does the existing codebase actually shorten each?

## Verdict

**Go — but on the channel, not the algorithm.** The decisive, founder-specific advantage is the **SDVOSB procurement preference**, layered on **competent AI back-office/operational tooling**, sold to **VA/VHA + veteran-serving SUD programs (#1)** and **private sober-living operators (#2)** — never to recovering members, and never in a structure that takes referral-tied remuneration (EKRA). Do **not** enter SUD ambient-scribe or generic operator-RCM head-on against Ritten/Alleva/Eleos.

> **Gating questions now resolved** (see `REPORT-procurement-validation.md`): VA demand is **real** (FY2024: $10.2B / 23% of prime dollars to SDVOSBs; a ~$5M sole-source SDVOSB mental-health-software award exists under NAICS 541511) but **lumpy and relationship-driven** — certification buys eligibility, not the award. Time-to-revenue: **direct-to-operator is faster** (HIPAA-only, no certification); the **VA track is floored at ~12–18+ months** behind two serial gates (SBA VetCert → VA ATO; FedRAMP is _not_ cleanly avoidable). So run **operator now, VA as the slower parallel moat-track**, using **SBIR/STTR and SDVOSB-prime subcontracting** as the solo on-ramps that sidestep the prime+ATO barrier.

**Sequenced entry plan:**

1. **De-risk the moat first (weeks 0–6, near-zero cost):** start **SBA VetCert SDVOSB certification** (gating step), and run **VA/VHA + SAM.gov market research** to confirm real solicitation demand for SUD/behavioral-health operational software — this single check decides whether #1 is real or theoretical. `[gating]`
2. **Pick the wedge product the codebase already half-builds:** scope an **operator-tier AI tool** (RATS census/compliance/alumni-outcomes, or peer-specialist supervision/QA) that is _not_ the EMR/scribe incumbents' lane — usable as both the VA offering and the commercial sober-living offering.
3. **Land commercial reference customers in parallel (months 1–4):** sell #2 to a handful of private sober-living operators via the warm lived-recovery network (CAC ≈ $0) to prove the product and outcomes while certification clears.
4. **Convert to the VA channel (months 3–12):** once VetCert-certified and demand is confirmed, pursue SDVOSB set-aside / sole-source work with the proven tool.
5. **Hold #3/#4 as upside** — add denials/UR features or a flat-fee licensed engagement-data layer only when a paying, EKRA-clean counterparty appears.
