## 0. The core insight

Your edges don't form one opportunity — they form **two distinct, separately
defensible moat-clusters**, each with its own market, buyer, and monetization surface:

- **Cluster A — Veteran / Federal:** _US veteran + SDVOSB-eligible + CGI Federal
  procurement literacy + ex-Meta AI engineering + having personally navigated VA
  disability._ This combination is genuinely rare — the pool of SBA-verified SDVOSBs who
  can credibly architect a RAG system AND speak federal-PM language is thin. It has **two
  reinforcing monetization surfaces**: SDVOSB AI federal services (fast cash) and
  VA-claims AI (sold to the VA itself or to accredited reps).
- **Cluster B — Recovery / Regulatory:** _lived addiction recovery (authentic operator
  access) + deep 42 CFR Part 2 / EKRA / HIPAA knowledge + a warm CPA channel into
  treatment centers._ This is the moat for the strongest **net-new product idea** found:
  compliance "picks & shovels" for SUD operators — where an actual **empty shelf** exists.

The strategic question isn't "which idea" — it's **which cluster to lead with given your
binding constraint (bandwidth, per `REPORT.md` §8).** Recommendation in §6.

## 2. SDVOSB AI federal services — the income engine (Rank #1)

**Why it's the fastest, most defensible cash.** The federal SDVOSB set-aside pool was
**$28.6B in FY2025**; the **VA awards ~23.6% of its contract dollars to SDVOSBs** —
4.6× the federal average — because _Kingdomware_ makes the "Rule of Two" set-aside
**mandatory and statutory** (insulated from DOGE/executive churn). Federal AI demand is
exploding (obligated AI funds $261M→$7.2B 2022→2026), VA runs the largest federal AI
footprint (367 use cases; $29M mission-AI line, +130% YoY), and small businesses are
winning the _integration/implementation_ work — exactly your skill set.

**The cold-start is solved by your CGI network.** A solo with no past performance wins
the first contract as a **subcontractor to a T4NG2/SPRUCE prime** (your labor counts
toward the prime's SDVOSB self-performance floor → they _want_ you). Then: VA
**sole-source up to $5M** (VAAR 819.7008, no past-performance requirement — just demonstrated
capability + a CO relationship), then prime. Realistic billing: **$130–175/hr**; **$200–500k
owner income yr1–2**, **$500k–$1M+ by yr3–4** with a small team.

**Monetization model:** high-margin professional services first; build FedRAMP-readiness
and a productized AI tool _alongside_ (SPRUCE is a 100% SDVOSB digital-services IDIQ built
for exactly this hybrid). Lifestyle-scale-to-small-firm, not venture SaaS — but the
structural moat is real and contracts are multi-year.

**Single best first move:** _this costs nothing._ Email/DM former CGI Federal colleagues
now at firms holding T4NG2 or SPRUCE prime slots: "I'm a verified SDVOSB AI engineer —
teaming with me counts toward your self-performance floor and strengthens your bid; let's
talk about the **June 2026 T4NG2 recompete wave**." Run **SBA VetCert + SAM.gov** in
parallel (60–90 days). _(Kill factors: VA-concentration risk; the 6-month BD dry spell
before first revenue; meticulous SDVOSB ownership/control compliance — a $21.3M fraud
settlement landed June 2026, so the legitimate profile is itself a moat.)_

## 3. SUD compliance platform (EKRA + Part 2) — the empty-shelf product (Rank #2)

**The standout finding: there is NO EKRA compliance software of any kind.** None on G2,
Capterra, or the healthcare-compliance market. EKRA exposure is **criminal** ($200k + 10
years _per occurrence_), covers **all payers** (not just federal), enforcement is
**accelerating** (the 9th Circuit's 2025 _Schena_ ruling + DOJ 2025 priorities), and
nearly every treatment center pays commission-based marketing/call-center comp without
documented FMV — i.e., latent criminal exposure across the whole industry. Today it's
served only by attorneys at $400–700/hr. **This is the single biggest gap across every
wedge researched.**

Pair it with the **42 CFR Part 2 compliance overlay**: the Feb-2024 Final Rule's
enforcement just fired (Feb 16, 2026, now under OCR with penalties to $73k/violation),
and most centers run EHRs (Kipu, Qualifacts, legacy) that don't produce an OCR-ready
disclosure audit trail. An **EHR-agnostic overlay** (consent management, minimum-necessary
disclosure log, re-disclosure tracking, breach-readiness) is a clean recurring-revenue gap.

**Why you specifically win:** lived-recovery credibility + genuine Part 2/EKRA depth +
the **CPA warm channel** = near-zero-CAC distribution. A CPA telling a treatment-center
owner "you have undocumented EKRA exposure" is far more potent than a cold demo — it's the
EKRA analogue of audit-risk advisory they already buy.

**Monetization:** EKRA — **$500–1,500/mo** monitoring SaaS + **$2–5k** one-time FMV
documentation engagements; Part 2 — **$200–500/mo** ($3–6k ACV). Compounded as a unified
**SUD regulatory-compliance platform** (EKRA + Part 2 + state-licensure calendar): no
competitor offers all three (Simplifyance is CARF-only and doesn't touch EKRA). 50
facilities × ~$750/mo ≈ **$450k ARR** before engagement fees; addressable upside into the
**$1–6M ARR** range via the channel.

**Two real watch-outs:** (1) **UPL risk** — the FMV/arrangement-legality opinion must be
attorney-backed; structure the software as documentation + risk-flagging (the Westlaw /
Compliancy-Group model), with a partner attorney signing opinions. (2) **Ongoing
regulatory-maintenance cost** — EKRA is interpretively early and Part 2 is mid-rollout;
your domain knowledge is the launch moat but staying current is a recurring cost (fund it
with per-customer pricing + an attorney advisory relationship). **First product
(rule-set risk-screener + template library + training-doc generator) is buildable in
60–90 days.**

## 4. VA-claims AI — high mission-fit, but B2B/B2G only (Rank #3)

**The market is enormous and growing** (~3M claims/yr, PACT-Act demand wave, $173B comp
flowing, a **$500M–$1B/yr** paid-help industry). And your fit is _exceptional_ — veteran +
having navigated the C-File/DBQ/secondary-condition maze yourself + federal systems
literacy + AI engineering. **But there is one existential constraint that dictates the
entire model.**

**38 U.S.C. § 5901/5904: a non-accredited entity cannot legally charge a veteran for
_initial_ claim preparation — at all.** The whole consumer claim-shark model (Trajector,
VA Claims Insider) is under active lawsuit, NPR/War Horse investigation, and state bans.
**Do not build the B2C contingency/flat-fee initial-claims product.** It will be sued out
of existence.

**The clean, founder-fit monetization paths:**

1. **B2B SaaS to the ~35k VA-accredited attorneys/claims agents** — they _can_ legally
   charge (post-decision, 20–33% of back-pay), so efficiency = margin. **No AI-native
   tool exists for them today (empty shelf).** $200–400/seat/mo; **10 beta firms × $500/mo
   = $60k ARR proof in <6 months**, bootstrappable. Near-zero regulatory risk (you're
   selling software to licensed pros, like Clio/Westlaw).
2. **B2G to the VA itself** — the VA is actively buying AI for claims processing; this
   **runs on the exact same SDVOSB vehicle and VA relationships as Rank #1**, so it
   compounds with the income engine rather than competing for bandwidth.

_(Kill factors: GUARD-Act-style legislation could sweep in veteran-facing software; the VA
could build/give away a consumer tool — both risks hit B2C, not the B2B-attorney or B2G
paths. VeteranAI already occupies the freemium B2C niche.)_

is distribution + embedded workflow knowledge + a live reference customer — not the tech.**

→ **Best path = productized _services_ (audit→sprint→retainer), then selectively
productize** what every client repeatedly uses. $5–15k sprints, $3.5–5k/mo retainers,
**$350k–$1.2M ARR solo** via the warm channel, 65–75% margins, 30–60-day warm sales cycle.
This is the same as portfolio #1/#2 — **research adds confidence, not a new idea.** Don't
build CPA SaaS as the first move.

## 6. Recommendation — what to actually do

**Lead with Cluster A as the engine; build Cluster B's empty-shelf product in parallel;
gate VA-claims to B2B/B2G.** Concretely, given bandwidth is the binding constraint:

1. **Primary income engine → SDVOSB AI federal services (#1).** It's the fastest path to
   real, multi-year, structurally-protected cash, and the first move (CGI-network teaming
   outreach) costs nothing and can be done this week alongside the CPA engine. This can
   _replace_ the "rank-#4, park-it" patience the BH-RCM brief was constrained by — federal
   money funds everything else.
2. **Highest-upside net-new product bet → SUD compliance platform, EKRA-first (#2).** It's
   the only genuine empty shelf found, it reuses your CPA channel and recovery moat, the
   MVP is a 60–90-day build, and it's a far cleaner monetization than BH-RCM (no
   patient-flow/EKRA-bridge exposure, criminal-risk-driven WTP). **Validate with 3–5 CPA
   treatment-center clients before building** (same gate discipline as BH-RCM).
3. **VA-claims AI (#3): pursue only as B2B-to-accredited-reps or B2G — never B2C.** The
   B2G version literally _is_ a deliverable inside #1, so it compounds rather than competes.
4. **CPA AI (#4): keep as the warm-channel _services_ engine you're already running** —
   don't divert into building CPA SaaS.

**The cross-cutting unlock for everything:** complete **SBA VetCert + SAM.gov** now. It's
table-stakes for #1, opens B2G for #3, costs little, and the "verified SDVOSB AI engineer"
identity is the rare credential that ties Cluster A together.

**Single highest-value next action:** one email to a former CGI Federal colleague about
SDVOSB teaming on the June-2026 T4NG2 recompete. Zero cost, can produce a paid subcontract
in 60–90 days, and it's the move only _you_ — among nearly all AI engineers — are
positioned to make.
