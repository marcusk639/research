## What it is

A focused review of how work actually moves through your firm — time entry, document
intake, knowledge base, billing, WIP — to pinpoint where staff hours leak and which
workflows are worth automating on the tools you already run (Karbon, QuickBooks,
SharePoint, Teams).

You get a prioritized, partner-readable findings report. No 100-slide deck, no
open-ended retainer to start. A fixed fee, a fixed timeline, a concrete answer.
