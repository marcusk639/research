# Advisory Revenue-Share Term Sheet — DRAFT

**Purpose:** Convert the current _verbal_ compensation understanding at TWK CPA Firm into a written,
metric-based agreement. The v2 research flagged this as the single highest-leverage action and the
biggest unmanaged risk in the entire portfolio — the income engine that funds everything else rests
on an unwritten deal.

> This is a plain-language term sheet to align on economics **before** a lawyer drafts the binding
> document. Bracketed values `[ ]` are the levers to negotiate; the notes give a recommended default
> and the rationale. Not legal advice — have counsel paper the final agreement.

## 1. Scope — what the share applies to

The revenue share covers **net new advisory & automation revenue** that Advisor materially sources,
builds, or delivers, in these categories:

- AI/automation/software engagements sold to **Firm clients** (the "advisory line").
- AI/automation/software sold to **non-accounting businesses** via Firm introductions.
- Recurring retainers, productized services, and any software/tooling the Advisor builds that the Firm bills for.

**Excluded:** ordinary tax-prep, bookkeeping, audit, and existing CAS revenue the Firm already earned
before this agreement (the baseline). The share is on the _incremental_ line Advisor creates.

## 2. The economics

| Lever                         | Recommended default                                                                        | Rationale                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Revenue-share rate**        | **[20%] of net advisory revenue collected**                                                | Net = collected cash less direct third-party costs (API/cloud/subcontractor). Pays on _collection_, not billing, so bad debt is shared. |
| **Alternative: margin split** | [35–50%] of _gross margin_ on the advisory line                                            | Use instead of top-line % if the Firm fronts meaningful cost. Cleaner alignment; harder to compute.                                     |
| **Base raise trigger**        | Base → $[65–75k] when advisory line clears $[X] cumulative collected, or at [12]-mo review | Replaces the vague "raise against ROI." Tie to a hard number, not "regularly."                                                          |
| **Floor / draw**              | Optional monthly draw of $[ ] against the share, trued-up quarterly                        | Smooths cash if engagements are lumpy.                                                                                                  |
| **Payment cadence**           | Quarterly, within [30] days of quarter close                                               |                                                                                                                                         |

## 3. Measurement & transparency (kills the "undefined ROI" risk)

- **System of record:** advisory revenue, costs, and attribution tracked in [Karbon / a shared sheet],
  reviewed jointly each quarter. Advisor gets read access.
- **Attribution rule:** an engagement counts toward the share if Advisor [sourced it OR scoped/built/delivered it].
  Edge cases (brother sources, Advisor delivers) split [50/50] of the share rate.
- **"ROI" defined:** ROI = (client-billed advisory revenue + documented Firm cost savings from internal
  automation) ÷ Advisor's fully-loaded cost. Reviewed quarterly against the base-raise trigger.

## 4. IP & ownership (the part that protects the _resell_ upside)

- Tools/software Advisor builds for the Firm: Firm gets a **perpetual internal-use license**.
- **Advisor retains ownership of the underlying IP, templates, and codebase** and the right to
  productize and resell it to other firms (the v2 "resell-to-other-CPA-firms" venture, rank #2).
- Client data stays the Firm's / clients'. Advisor's reusable framework is Advisor's.
- _This single clause is what keeps the #2 venture alive — do not sign it away._

## 5. Standard protections

- **Termination:** either party, [60] days' notice. On termination, Advisor is paid the share on all
  revenue collected for engagements Advisor sourced/built for [12] months ("tail").
- **Confidentiality / client non-solicit:** mutual, reasonable, time-boxed.
- **Dispute resolution:** good-faith discussion → mediation before any formal action.
- **Family clause:** because the parties are brothers, both agree the _written_ terms govern over any
  prior verbal understanding, specifically to protect the relationship.

## 6. Open items to settle before signing

1. Exact share rate (top-line % vs margin split) and base-raise trigger number.
2. Attribution split for jointly-sourced deals.
3. Whether non-accounting-business revenue is in scope or a separate arrangement.
4. Confirm IP/resell clause is acceptable to the Firm.
5. Have a Louisiana attorney convert this into a binding independent-contractor or
   employment-plus-incentive agreement.
