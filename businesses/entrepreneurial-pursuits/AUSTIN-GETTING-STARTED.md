## The two tracks (run in parallel — they don't compete for the same hours early on)

- **Track 1 — SDVOSB AI federal-services engine.** Fastest cash, lowest regulatory risk,
  runs on gov cloud (no hardware). The Austin/Texas support infrastructure for this is
  excellent and almost entirely **free**.
- **Track 2 — SUD compliance product (EKRA + Part 2) validation.** Your empty-shelf
  product bet. Early work is _legal gate + design-partner interviews_, not building.
  Austin has 100+ local treatment centers and Austin-anchored provider associations.

`recovery-platform` is the **funding + credibility** underneath both — and its immediate
job is _activation_ (turn on Stripe prices, test Regroup payment paths) to capture the
revenue you already have demand for.

## First 60 days — sequenced

### Week 1 — Federal foundation (free, do these yourself, online)

1. **Register on SAM.gov** → [sam.gov](https://sam.gov). Get your UEI. Required for all
   federal (and a prerequisite for VetCert). Pick NAICS **541511, 541512, 541519, 541690**.
2. **Apply for SDVOSB VetCert** → [certifications.sba.gov](https://certifications.sba.gov/),
   help line **866-722-4357**. Free. Have DD-214 + VA disability rating ready. (~12-day
   processing now.) _This is the single credential that unlocks Track 1._
3. **Apply for Texas VetHUB** (state-level SDVOSB analog, now SDV-exclusive) →
   [vethub.gob2g.com](https://vethub.gob2g.com/), **VetHUBProgram@cpa.texas.gov**,
   512-463-5872. Free, runs concurrently. Requires ≥20% VA rating + 51% ownership +
   Texas-based.

### Week 2 — Get a free government-contracting counselor + legal gate started

4. **UTSA APEX Accelerator — Austin office** (free govcon counseling; the most useful
   single Track-1 resource): **(210) 458-3353**, apexaccelerator@utsa.edu, Austin office
   _2525 W. Anderson Ln., Ste. 365, Austin 78757_ **[re-verify — main site was erroring]**.
   Ask for: NAICS/SAM review, **capability statement**, and bid-match alerts for SDVOSB
   set-asides.
5. **UT Arlington VBOC** (free veteran business counseling, covers Central TX):
   **(817) 272-6789**, utavboc@uta.edu. Ask for **Boots to Business: Reboot** and VA
   contracting advising.
6. **Track 2 legal gate — call the EKRA/Part 2 attorneys** (don't touch patient data
   before this): **Rivas Goldstein** (Austin-local, names EKRA defense) **800-761-5190**,
   _3345 Bee Cave Rd Ste 104, Austin 78746_; and **Benkoff Health Law** (national; the one
   firm covering EKRA + HIPAA + 42 CFR Part 2 + QSOA together) **248-482-2780**. Ask for: a
   **QSOA template**, and a written opinion on vendor-side EKRA/UPL risk for a compliance SaaS.

### Week 3 — State + SBA navigators, and build your Track-2 prospect list

7. **Texas Veterans Commission — Veterans Entrepreneur Program**: **(512) 463-0519**,
   vep@tvc.texas.gov. Ask for the **Central Texas regional consultant** (advises on both
   VetHUB state contracting and federal strategy).
8. **SBA San Antonio District — Veteran Business Development Officer** (covers Austin):
   **Don Owens [re-verify]**, don.owens@sba.gov, (210) 403-5912. Ask about VA procurement
   forecasts + upcoming SDVOSB matchmaking events.
9. **Pull your Track-2 prospect list** (free, ground truth): **HHSC Chemical Dependency
   Treatment Facility license database** (filter Travis/Hays/Williamson/Bastrop counties) →
   [hhs.texas.gov](https://www.hhs.texas.gov/providers/health-care-facilities-regulation/chemical-dependency-treatment-facilities),
   cross-referenced with [findtreatment.gov/locator](https://findtreatment.gov/locator).
   That's every licensed SUD facility within 30 miles of you.

### Week 4 — Plug into the rooms that matter

10. **Capital Factory — Monthly Military Connected Mentor Meetup** (701 Brazos St; the
    highest-density veteran-founder + defense-tech network in Austin). Bring a one-paragraph
    capability pitch + your SAM UEI. Ask about the **Dual-Use Academy** (govt-sales path).
11. **ASAP Texas** (statewide SUD _facility_ trade association, Austin office): **830-792-4541**,
    [asaptexas.org](https://www.asaptexas.org/) — and **BHAT** (Austin private-provider
    coalition): **512-775-9854**, Courtney Williamson, courtney@wrighttexas.com. Ask for
    **design-partner introductions** (discovery interviews), not sales meetings.

### Ongoing

- Weekly: scan the **VA Forecast of Opportunities**
  ([vip-vems.powerappsportals.us/forecast](https://vip-vems.powerappsportals.us/forecast/))
  and SAM.gov SDVOSB set-aside alerts in your NAICS codes.
- The **highest-leverage Track-1 move** is still warm: reach former **CGI Federal**
  colleagues now at firms holding **T4NG2 / SPRUCE** prime slots and offer SDVOSB teaming
  for the **June-2026 T4NG2 recompete**. Find Austin-area SDVOSB primes via SAM.gov entity
  search (e.g., **Stellar Technology** in Cedar Park, **ATX Defense** in Austin — verify
  status on SAM.gov).

## recovery-platform — the parallel "finish what you have" task

Before layering AI on it, the highest-ROI engineering is **activation**: set Stripe default
prices (Homegroups checkout currently throws), raise **Regroup payment/auth test coverage**
off ~5%, and finish Detox email/PDF fulfillment. That converts existing demand into recorded
MRR — which is exactly the _active business income_ that makes a Section 179 / bonus-depreciation
hardware write-off useful later. AI features here stay on **cheap cloud models**, not your GPU.

## What to decide / confirm (you, this week)

1. **Is your SDVOSB paperwork (DD-214 + rating docs) in hand?** Everything in Track 1 gates on it.
2. **Do you still have warm CGI Federal contacts** at prime-holding firms? That's the Track-1 cold-start unlock.
3. **Current recovery-platform MRR + which product** — sets how much income exists to expense hardware against this tax year (confirm treatment with your CPA).
4. **Budget for the legal gate** (~a few $k for QSOA + EKRA opinion) before any treatment-center pilot.
